Critical Interoperability Gaps
AnonCreds ≠ OID4VP (today)
CREDEBL's ZKP capability only works over DIDComm. No standard OID4VP path yet. ETA: 12–24 months.
QuarkID's WACI-DIDComm divergence
WACI-DIDComm is not directly compatible with OID4VP verifiers. Bridge required for global interop.
Apple Wallet: mDoc-only (no OID4VP)
Safari DC API supports org-iso-mdoc only. SD-JWT VC and W3C VC cannot be presented via Safari today.
Bridge pattern — proven in production
OID4VC ↔ DIDComm hybrid possible: DIDComm endpoint embedded in DID after OID4VC handshake. Demonstrated: IATA One ID (Oct 2024).
DPG PlatformsArchitecture & sweet spot
INJI (MOSIP)
MOSIP · Open-source DPG
OID4VCI · OID4VP · SD-JWT VC · ISO 18013-5
🎯 Government-scale national credential programs. 100M+ IDs in production across 10+ countries. OID4VC + mDL native. Components: Inji Wallet, Inji Web, Inji Certify, Inji Verify.
CREDEBL (AYANWORKS)
AYANWORKS · LFDT · Hyperledger
DIDComm v1/v2 · AnonCreds · OID4VC · SD-JWT VC
🎯 ZKP-max privacy use cases. Only DPG with production AnonCreds predicates. Built on Credo (ex-Aries Framework JS). Dual-stack: DIDComm legacy + OID4VC modern path.
walt.id
walt.id GmbH · EUDIW-aligned
OID4VCI · OID4VP · SIOPv2 · W3C VC · SD-JWT VC · mDoc
🎯 EU regulatory contexts and multi-format wallets. Most ARF/HAIP-aligned platform. Simultaneous support for all major formats. Best fit for EUDIW pilots.
QuarkID
Buenos Aires · Extrimian · LATAM
WACI-DIDComm · W3C VC · zkSync L2 · BBS+
🎯 LATAM government digital identity. Blockchain-anchored trust (zkSync). Production in Buenos Aires. DIDComm-primary — OID4VC bridge needed for global interop.
